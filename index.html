所有护理工作都要做背景调查 — 您要知道什么 All caregiver jobs require background checks — what to expect
他们会查什么？What They Check
  • FBI 指纹（LiveScan）FBI fingerprints (LiveScan)
  • 加州犯罪记录California criminal record
  • 虐待老人/儿童记录Elder/child abuse records
  • 护理执照核实Caregiver license verification
  • 结核病 (TB) 测试TB (tuberculosis) test
什么会被拒？What Disqualifies You
  • 严重暴力犯罪（10 年内）Serious violent crimes (within 10 yrs)
  • 虐待老人或儿童的记录Elder or child abuse
  • 医保诈骗（Medi-Cal fraud）Medi-Cal / healthcare fraud
  • 毒品贩卖（不是吸毒）Drug trafficking (not drug use)
  • 性犯罪记录Sexual offenses
💡 移民身份不影响背景调查 — 没有合法身份也可以做家庭护理 (IHSS 例外) 💡 Immigration status does NOT affect background checks — you can work in many caregiving jobs without legal status (IHSS is an exception)
